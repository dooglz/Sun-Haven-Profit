      How recommendations are calculated

      Craft values are extra sale value per batch after subtracting every ingredient’s resale value. Mixed currencies, missing prices, and uncertain fish eligibility are left out of the recommendation.

      How prices are compared · Data sources

      Bracketed prices show what the listed quantity would sell for. Extra per item = (batch sale − total ingredient resale value) ÷ number of the selected item used. Mana and health have no sale value. A rarity recipe assumes every fish used in its batch is the fish you picked.

      Uses base wiki prices and direct recipes. The maxed Seed Maker skill adds one seed per batch at the Seed Maker and Elven Seed Maker. Other skill bonuses, processing chains, and non-crafting uses are outside the calculation. Recipes with mixed currencies, missing prices, or uncertain fish eligibility are shown without a profit rank.
